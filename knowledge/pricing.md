# PrimeSoul Web Solutions — Pricing Models & Guidelines

## 1. Core Pricing Philosophy
- **Value-Anchored**: Pricing is tied to the measurable business impact, complexity, and revenue growth unlocked for the client, not arbitrary hours.
- **Transparent & Modular**: Clear deliverables with defined scopes, avoiding hidden costs or vague estimations.
- **Rule Against Invention**: AI agents must never fabricate specific discounted rates, fake guarantees, or unauthorized pricing. When generating proposals, use the standard tiers below or insert clear placeholder ranges for final human review.

## 3. Proposal Pricing Rules for AI Agents
1. Always state whether taxes (e.g. GST) and third-party fees (domains, hosting, ad spend) are included or separate.
2. In all initial proposals, label pricing as: *"Estimated Investment Tier (Subject to Final Scope Confirmation)"*.
3. Require Human Approval before sending any binding proposal document.
