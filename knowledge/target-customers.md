# PrimeSoul Web Solutions — Target Customers & ICPs

## 1. Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs)

### ICP 1: Local & Regional Service Businesses
- **Firmographics**: 5–50 employees, annual revenue ₹50L - ₹5Cr / $100K - $1M.
- **Decision Makers**: Business Owner, Managing Director, Practice Head.
- **Pain Points**: Outdated or broken website, invisible on Google Maps, losing local customers to competitors, zero automated follow-up.
- **Buying Triggers**: Launching a new clinic/branch, rebranding, negative feedback from customers about their website.
- **Primary Solutions**: Website Redesign, Google Business Profile Optimization, Local SEO, WhatsApp Business Automation.

### ICP 2: Growing D2C & E-Commerce Brands
- **Firmographics**: 2–20 team members, monthly revenue ₹5L - ₹50L.
- **Decision Makers**: Founder, E-Commerce Manager, Head of Growth.
- **Pain Points**: Poor mobile conversion rates, slow Shopify/WooCommerce store, high Meta Ads acquisition costs, abandoned carts.
- **Buying Triggers**: Scaling ad spend, seasonal product drops, expanding SKU catalogs.
- **Primary Solutions**: High-Speed Custom Storefront, Landing Page Optimization, Meta Ads Management, Performance SEO.

### ICP 3: B2B Enterprises & Consultancies
- **Firmographics**: 10–100 employees, B2B services, consulting, IT, logistics.
- **Decision Makers**: CEO, VP of Sales, Marketing Director.
- **Pain Points**: Lengthy sales cycles, low lead volume, manual spreadsheet tracking, lack of digital authority.
- **Buying Triggers**: Hiring sales reps, entering new markets, launching a new B2B offering.
- **Primary Solutions**: Custom Web Applications, Google Search Ads, PrimeOMS SaaS Implementation, LinkedIn Content Strategy.

---

## 2. ICP Disqualifiers (Accounts to Filter Out)
- Zero budget or seeking 100% free/commission-only work without upfront commitment.
- Businesses with no active decision-maker available for discovery.
- Niches violating platform policies (e.g. deceptive schemes, unauthorized services).
