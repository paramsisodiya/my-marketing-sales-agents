# PrimeSoul Web Solutions — Proprietary Products & Platforms

PrimeSoul Web Solutions develops internal software platforms and SaaS solutions to provide operational leverage for clients.

---

## 1. PrimeOMS (Operations & Management System)
- **Category**: Custom Enterprise Operations & Business Management SaaS.
- **Core Value Proposition**: Unifies lead management, client onboarding, project milestone tracking, billing, and team collaboration into a single, cohesive dashboard.
- **Key Modules**:
  - *Lead Pipeline & Deal Tracker*: Visual Kanban board for qualification and stage progression.
  - *Order & Service Fulfillment*: Workflow checklists for website delivery, SEO sprint tasks, and ad campaigns.
  - *Client Portal*: Self-service dashboard for clients to review deliverables, invoices, and performance reports.
  - *Automated Invoicing & Payments*: Payment gateway integration, invoice generation, and recurring subscription tracking.
- **Target Audience**: Digital agencies, professional service firms, consultancies, and multi-location businesses.
- **Deployment**: Available as a cloud-hosted solution with custom white-labeling capabilities.

## 2. PrimeSoul AI (Operating System)
