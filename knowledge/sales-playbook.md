# PrimeSoul Web Solutions — Sales Playbook & Methodologies

## 1. Sales Philosophy: Signal-Based Consultative Selling
PrimeSoul does not execute mass spray-and-pray outbound. Every sales interaction is initiated by an observable trigger (e.g., poor website speed, unverified Google Business Profile, broken mobile responsiveness, hiring surges, competitor advertising).

---

## 2. Multi-Channel Outreach Sequences (8–10 Touches over 3 Weeks)
- **Touch 1 (Day 1 - Email)**: Signal-based opener + quantified problem observation + soft CTA.
- **Touch 2 (Day 3 - LinkedIn / WhatsApp)**: Low-friction connection with specific insight (no aggressive pitch).
- **Touch 3 (Day 6 - Email)**: Value teardown / short actionable recommendation.
- **Touch 4 (Day 9 - Phone / Voicemail)**: Friendly reference to previous email insight.
- **Touch 5 (Day 13 - WhatsApp / Email)**: Case study archetype demonstrating similar bottleneck resolution.
- **Touch 6 (Day 17 - Email)**: Alternative angle — addressing business operational cost or lost local search visibility.
- **Touch 7 (Day 21 - Email)**: Breakup email — polite, leaving the door open for future growth cycles.

---

## 3. Discovery Call Architecture (30 Minutes)

### Upfront Contract (First 2 Minutes)
*"Thanks for connecting today. My goal for our 30 minutes is to dive into your current digital infrastructure and see if there is a genuine fit for PrimeSoul to help you scale. At the end, we'll either agree there's a strong alignment and map next steps, or conclude we aren't the best fit right now — both outcomes are completely fine. Does that sound fair?"*

### Discovery Methodologies
1. **SPIN Selling**:
   - *Situation*: Understand current tools, traffic, and team workflow (keep to max 2–3 questions).
   - *Problem*: Surface bottlenecks (e.g., "Where are website visitors dropping off?").
   - *Implication*: Uncover downstream cost of inaction (e.g., "If your site stays slow, how many patient bookings do you estimate are lost each month?").
   - *Need-Payoff*: Let buyer state value (e.g., "If you could automate lead capture into WhatsApp, how much time would your front desk save?").
2. **Gap Selling**:
   - Map Current State (tools, lost revenue, slow load times) vs. Desired Future State (top 3 ranking, automated intake, modern branding). The gap represents the sale.
3. **Sandler Pain Funnel**:
   - Level 1: Surface technical complaint ("Site looks dated").
   - Level 2: Business revenue impact ("Losing deals to new clinic down the street").
   - Level 3: Personal/Stakeholder impact ("Frustrated by spending on ads that don't convert").

## 4. Deal Qualification: MEDDPICC Framework
Every qualified deal in pipeline must be assessed against:
- **M - Metrics**: Quantifiable outcome (e.g., 25+ qualified inquiries/month).
- **E - Economic Buyer**: The person controlling financial spend.
- **D - Decision Criteria**: Technical and business requirements for vendor choice.
- **D - Decision Process**: Exact steps from proposal review to sign-off.
- **P - Paper Process**: Contracts, invoice terms, advance payment requirements.
- **I - Identify Pain**: Root cause business pain and cost of doing nothing.
- **C - Champion**: Internal advocate who supports PrimeSoul's engagement.
- **C - Competition**: Alternatives (in-house, competitor agency, do nothing).

---

## 5. Objection Handling (AECR Framework)
- **A - Acknowledge**: Validate the concern ("I completely understand budget predictability is crucial").
- **E - Empathize**: Normalize ("Many business owners we work with initially had the same concern").
- **C - Clarify**: Ask diagnostic question ("Is the concern the total investment, or confidence in the timeline to return on that investment?").
- **R - Reframe**: Provide strategic perspective anchored in the value equation.
